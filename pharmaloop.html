A buyer-first process
built around trust.

PharmaLoop aims to secure buyer demand first, then purchase eligible inventory from the supplier and resell it under PharmaLoop's own commercial terms.

01

Submit surplus or demand

Suppliers share stock details privately. Buyers send requirements without seeing the source company.

02

Verify the opportunity

We review specifications, availability, ownership evidence and category-specific compliance requirements.

03

Confirm both sides

Buyer terms and a firm supplier offer are aligned before PharmaLoop commits to purchase.

04

Purchase, resell and deliver

Orders, invoices, payment, dispatch and traceability records are managed for the transaction.
