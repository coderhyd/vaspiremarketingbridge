Brand fonts

The original card uses Calibre Regular and Calibre Semibold (Klim Type Foundry). Use your licensed copies for an exact match. They stay in this browser tab only (re-upload after reloading the page). Without them, Barlow Semi Condensed is used — its letter widths are within ~1–3 % of Calibre.

Option 1 — Fonts installed on this computer

Works in Chrome or Edge on Windows/Mac. The browser will ask permission to see your installed fonts. Note: Microsoft Calibri is a different font from Klim Calibre.

Option 2 — Upload font files

On Windows the files are in C:\Windows\Fonts (all users) or %LOCALAPPDATA%\Microsoft\Windows\Fonts (current user). Copy them to the Desktop first if the picker shows the Fonts folder as icons.

      Alignment rules used by the generator

      • Outer margins ≈ 6 mm: logo left 6.07, QR right 5.99, back text left 5.76 mm.
      • Front text column starts at 42.73 mm (≈ half the card width). Name block is top-anchored at the 9.36 mm baseline; designation flows down at 9 pt leading.
      • Contact block is bottom-anchored: the email baseline stays at 49.09 mm, a second number (Direct) stacks upward.
      • Common bottom line ≈ 49.1 mm on both sides: front email baseline (49.09), back website baseline (49.03) and QR bottom edge (49.15).
      • Back address block is bottom-anchored 16 pt above the website line; the company name cap height lines up with the QR top (25.66 mm) when there are 5 address lines.
      • Text wider than its column wraps (designation, address) or shrinks down to 7 pt (name, phone, email, website). Front column max width 36.3 mm; back column max width 47.3 mm (6 mm gap before the QR).
      • Colours are CMYK: K100 name, K80 body text, C91 M79 Y62 K97 rich black for company name and QR — exactly as in the original file.
